SCR framework · Governing thought first 2 /

Cash is trapped in operations just as the top line softens — making working capital a board-level cash lever

Structural drivers

  • Manufacturing depth — Raw materials, work-in-progress, finished goods, in-transit
  • Commodity intensity — Price spikes inflate inventory dollars and cover-day habits
  • Long commercial cycles — Business-to-business credit and offtaker settlement lags
  • Fragmented ownership — Inventory, credit, and procurement optimized in silos

Why the timing matters

When revenue declines ~4–5% CAGR and day-metrics stay flat, Net Working Capital as % of revenue rises even if absolute dollars drift only modestly.
